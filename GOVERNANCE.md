# Governance

## Roles

- **Users** use odcr and report issues.
- **Contributors** submit pull requests, docs, reviews or issues.
- **Maintainers** review and merge pull requests, triage issues and cut releases. Listed in
  [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
- **Project lead** sets the roadmap and makes the final call when consensus cannot be reached.

## Decisions

- Small changes: one maintainer approval.
- New adapters, new commands and changes to the meta tables or the public Python API: an RFC issue, open for at
  least 7 days, then two maintainer approvals.
- Breaking changes are announced in the changelog one minor version before they happen whenever possible.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors with a sustained record of good pull requests and reviews can be nominated by a maintainer and are
added after approval by the existing maintainers.

## Code of conduct

Everyone follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
